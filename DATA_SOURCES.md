- Catalog: https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/mars_2020_science_investigation_ctx_dem_mosaic
- Direct GeoTIFF: https://planetarymaps.usgs.gov/mosaic/mars2020_trn/CTX/ScienceInvestigationMaps_JPL/M20_JezeroCrater_CTXDEM_20m.tif
- Catalog extent approximately lon 76.99–78.58 E, lat 17.58–19.29 N (catalog min/max latitude fields appear reversed; verify actual raster). Equirectangular, planetocentric latitude, positive-east longitude; catalog sphere radius 3396160 m. Read actual CRS rather than hard-code.
- Matched 5 m orthomosaic referenced by catalog; locate exact downloadable asset through catalog links/labels, not a guessed URL.

## Required preprocessing
1. Download, record source URL/time, file hash and product label; run `gdalinfo -json` or Rasterio inspection. Verify CRS, affine, units, pixel size, dimensions, mask/nodata, extent and plausible numeric samples.
2. Transform all footprints into common Mars coordinates and choose pilot intersection. Crop in projected metres; preserve CRS and nodata. Align analytical rasters; use nearest-neighbor for categorical layers. No Earth CRS shortcuts.
3. Derive slope using elevation gradient and true horizontal metres in local projected grid; check projection scale and use masks around missing neighbors. Keep native resolution or document resampling.
4. Generate lightweight outputs and manifest: product ID, instrument/mission, publisher, source URL, publication/acquisition dates if known, resolution, CRS, extent, units, nodata, license, checksum, processing steps and status (`catalog`, `downloaded`, `validated`, `derived`, `unavailable`). Show provenance in UI.

### E1 — MEDA / Perseverance / Mars 2020 (M4)
[PDS instrument/archive guide](https://pds-atmospheres.nmsu.edu/data_and_services/atmospheres_data/PERSEVERANCE/meda.html), [bundle](https://atmos.nmsu.edu/PDS/data/PDS4/Mars2020/mars2020_meda/), [bundle README](https://atmos.nmsu.edu/PDS/data/PDS4/Mars2020/mars2020_meda/readme.txt). Identifier `urn:nasa:pds:mars2020_meda`; calibrated environment collection `data_calibrated_env`. Science time series are ASCII CSV with PDS4 XML labels; SkyCam uses binary images. Sensors cover air/surface temperature, pressure, humidity, wind and optical radiation/dust. [PDS Analyst's Notebook collection descriptions](https://an.rsl.wustl.edu/help/Content/About%20the%20data/Data%20sets/M20/MEDA%20bundle.htm) identify air temperature in K, pressure in mbar, wind m/s, humidity %RH and solar flux W/m². Coverage is Perseverance's changing Jezero rover location, observations from February 19, 2021 onward as released, not a 5 km raster. Cadence is sensor/product/schedule dependent: inspect timestamps and quality flags; no fixed cadence validated here. Require UTC/sol and ancillary rover position before mapping. Historical samples cannot become regional present-day weather or storm forecasts. RDS optical/UV/IR measurements are not ionizing radiation dose. **Status:** archive documentation verified; no numeric product imported or spatially validated. Direct local README fetch failed/timed out; do not claim cached data.

### E2 — MARCI / MRO (M4)
PDS dataset [`MRO-M-MARCI-2-EDR-L0-V1.0`](https://pds.nasa.gov/ds-view/pds/viewDataset.jsp?dsid=MRO-M-MARCI-2-EDR-L0-V1.0), DOI [10.17189/1520403](https://doi.org/10.17189/1520403). [Exact product SIS](https://planetarydata.jpl.nasa.gov/img/data/mars%20reconnaissance%20orbiter/marci/mrom_0238/document/marcisis.pdf). Raw uncalibrated `.IMG` products have attached PDS3 labels; visible and UV files are separate. SIS gives ~1 km/pixel visible sampling at nadir from 300 km; UV is summed, off-nadir scale varies. Global orbital swaths enable broad daily monitoring; released [MSSS weather reports](https://www.msss.com/msss_images/subject/weather_reports.html) are dated visual/narrative context, not numeric local forecasts. Need calibration, SPICE geometry and actual time/footprint before a spatial dust product. Cloud/dust appearance is not local surface wind, quantitative opacity or a future hazard boundary. **Status:** dataset/SIS/report sources verified; no Jezero swath or georeferenced dust product acquired. Future panel may show historical regional context; no synthetic storm overlay.

### E3 — RAD / Curiosity / MSL (M4 reference only)
PDS PPI dataset [`MSL-M-RAD-3-RDR-V1.0`](https://search-pdsppi.igpp.ucla.edu/search/view/?f=null&id=pds%3A%2F%2FPPI%2FMSL-M-RAD-3-RDR-V1.0), DOI [10.17189/1519761](https://doi.org/10.17189/1519761). [RDR SIS v3.2](https://pds-ppi.igpp.ucla.edu/data/MSL-M-RAD-3-RDR-V1.0/DOCUMENT/RAD_RDR_SIS.PDF) documents per-sol `RAD_RDR_YYYY_DDD_HH_MM_SSSS_VNN.TXT` products, PDS labels, observation start/stop and dosimetry/histogram/counter/PHA sections. It includes cruise and **Gale Crater surface** operations; select surface-only records. Resolution is a rover-local instrument time series, not geographic pixels. Observation duration and normalization must be read from individual records; no fixed temporal sampling is asserted. Detector absorbed dose, dose rate and dose equivalent are distinct; preserve native units and calibration, never convert to astronaut exposure without an explicit model. No Jezero measurement or spatial radiation map follows from Gale data. **Status:** archive and format investigated, no numeric RAD import; allow only clearly titled historical Gale reference context.

### S1 — CRISM / MRO (M5)
[PDS source and current product families](https://pds-geosciences.wustl.edu/missions/mro/crism.htm), [exact MTRDR bundle](https://pds-geosciences.wustl.edu/mro/mro-m-crism-5-rdr-mptargeted-v1/mrocr_4001/), [bundle README](https://pds-geosciences.wustl.edu/mro/mro-m-crism-5-rdr-mptargeted-v1/mrocr_4001/readme.txt), [product SIS](https://pds-geosciences.wustl.edu/mro/mro-m-crism-2-edr-v1/mrocr_0002/document/crism_dpsis.htm). The PDS4 migration retains PDS3 files and adds XML labels. Products include binary hyperspectral image cubes, spectral-summary/browse products and wavelength tables. FRT nominal sampling is ~18 m/pixel at 300 km; HRL/HRS ~36 m, with actual projected scale in each label. Targeted scenes are discontinuous, not blanket Jezero coverage. MTRDR products correct/project targeted spectra; summary parameters indicate absorption behavior, not confirmed concentration/deposits. Need an exact observation ID and intersecting footprint from ODE, band/atmosphere/artifact checks and published interpretation. **Status:** product families/formats verified, no selected pilot-intersecting CRISM cube/label validated. NASA's [Jezero mineral illustration](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/images/pia23380-jezero-crater-minerals/) supports published regional context, not a georeferenced resource raster.

### S2 — USGS Jezero/Nili geology, SIM 3464 (M5)
Sun & Stack (2020), scale **1:75,000**, [DOI/catalog](https://doi.org/10.3133/sim3464). Source-linked exact [database ZIP](https://pubs.usgs.gov/sim/3464/sim3464_database.zip), [README](https://pubs.usgs.gov/sim/3464/sim3464_readme.txt), [FGDC XML](https://pubs.usgs.gov/sim/3464/sim3464_metadata.xml), [pamphlet](https://pubs.usgs.gov/sim/3464/sim3464_pamphlet.pdf). Metadata bounds **76.8–77.8°E, 17.7–18.7°N**. Mars Transverse Mercator, central meridian 77.5°E, sphere R=3396190 m, projected metres. Publication scale is not 20 m scientific precision. Crucially, README warns of **random 100–200 m offsets** versus the controlled CTX surface-operations mosaic. Do not auto-shift or imply exact contact alignment. **Status:** exact downloadable package/format/coverage verified via source metadata; ZIP/features not yet downloaded/read. Pilot intersection only catalog-level. Units are interpretive geology, not extractable water/mineral inventories.
